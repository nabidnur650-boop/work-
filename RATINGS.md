## ShiftTitan boundary

The 8.71 rating is justified by frozen protocols, task-disjoint replication,
exact reservation theory, pathwise invariants, family sensitivity, source-level
artifacts, deterministic builds, and a polished manuscript. Empirical
superiority is weaker: the two frozen panels fail their win-consistency
conjunctions, and the fresh confidence interval includes zero. Primary
empirical claim strength is rated 6.4/10.

## EviAudio boundary

The 8.95 rating is justified by controlled confirmation, source-disjoint
external evaluation, an all-video natural test, cap reconstruction, a
CLAP-only decomposition, paired bootstraps, a downstream bridge, retained
invalidations, and complete audit artifacts. Fixed QCR harms frozen natural
mAP, the cross-fit router is post outcome, and the learned answer bridge
fails. Overall multistage evidence strength is 7.8/10; a positive fixed
natural zero-shot claim is only 2.5/10.

No rating guarantees Q1 acceptance, and independent peer review remains
outstanding.
