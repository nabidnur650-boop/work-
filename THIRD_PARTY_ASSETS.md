# Third-party assets intentionally excluded

This repository does not redistribute:

- FEV benchmark datasets or Chronos-family weights;
- Perception Test provider audio and official source archives;
- LAION-CLAP weights;
- official ActionFormer features/checkpoint;
- UrbanSound8K, ESC-50, or LibriSpeech audio;
- large embedding/precompute archives; or
- the ten regenerable EviAudio candidate pools.

The study folders retain provider URLs, revisions, ordered identifiers,
checksums, licenses/rights notes, acquisition instructions, candidate-pool
row counts and hashes, and final routed predictions. Users remain responsible
for provider terms and attribution.
